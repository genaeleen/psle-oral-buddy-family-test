Quick refresher

Public spaces work well when we notice how our actions affect others.

  • MRT: notice passengers who need help, offer a seat and share limited space.
  • Library: speak softly, respect readers and accept polite reminders.
  • Hawker centre: return used trays, bin rubbish and consider the next diner.

Why it matters: safety, comfort, cleanliness and respect. A useful answer links an action to its effect and what you learnt.
